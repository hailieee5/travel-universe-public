# Munich & day-trip research pack

This is reference material for a future Munich illustration map. It does **not** prescribe a map design and does not add any visual asset.

## Spatial story to preserve

| Cluster | Existing photo folders | Map relationship |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Historic Munich core | `MUNICH/玛丽亚广场`, `老彼得教堂`, `阿萨姆教堂`, `慕尼黑王宫花园`, `慕尼黑工业大学` | Compact centre: St. Peter is directly behind Marienplatz; Asam Church sits southwest; Hofgarten is north-east. The supplied regional map identifies the Munich TUM campus as landmark #7. |
| Museum / Isar side | `MUNICH/老绘画博物馆`, `巴伐利亚国家博物馆` | Alte Pinakothek is northwest of the core; the National Museum is east, close to the Isar. |
| Railway / evening city | `MUNICH/哈克桥车站` | West of the historic core, at the station / Oktoberfest side of the city. |
| Freising / Weihenstephan | `Freising/慕尼黑工业大学（freising)`, `Freising/weihenstephaner啤酒厂` | North-east of Munich, sharing the Weihenstephan hill area. The supplied regional map identifies the Freising TUM campus as landmark #9. |
| Alpine day trips | `Füssen : Schwangau/福森`, `天鹅堡`, `奥地利-罗伊特` | Far south-west: Füssen is west of Neuschwanstein; Reutte lies farther south-west across the Austrian border. |

## Landmark reference notes

| Place | Useful visual / narrative cue | Primary reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marienplatz | Historic-centre anchor and visual starting point for the compact core. | [Munich Tourism city map](https://www.muenchen.travel/content/download/102841/file/260302_M%C3%BCnchen-Stadtplan-DE-Ansicht.pdf?inLanguage=ger-DE&version=36) |
| St. Peter’s Church | The oldest church in Munich; its tower is a strong vertical lookout cue immediately behind Marienplatz. | [Munich Tourism: Alter Peter](https://www.muenchen.travel/pois/stadt-viertel/alter-peter) |
| Asam Church | Dense late-Baroque façade; ideal for a richly ornamented, narrow-street sticker. | [Munich Tourism: Asamkirche](https://www.muenchen.travel/pois/stadt-viertel/asamkirche) |
| Hofgarten | Formal green garden on the edge of the old town, framed by arcades. | [Munich Tourism: Hofgarten](https://www.muenchen.travel/pois/stadt-viertel/hofgarten) |
| Hackerbrücke | Railway bridge, sunset and Oktoberfest-arrival atmosphere rather than a monumental building. | [Munich Tourism: bridges](https://www.muenchen.travel/artikel/stadt-viertel/bruecken-muenchen) |
| Alte Pinakothek | Monumental art-museum façade; the official site lists it at Barer Straße 27. | [Alte Pinakothek](https://www.pinakothek.de/en/alte-pinakothek) |
| Bavarian National Museum | Large museum on Prinzregentenstraße; suitable as an east-of-centre cultural landmark. | [Bayerisches Nationalmuseum](https://www.bayerisches-nationalmuseum.de/en) |
| TUM Munich Campus | Separate photo collection from Freising; represented by #7 on the supplied Munich / Bavaria map. | Supplied map and photo-folder audit |
| TUM Freising-Weihenstephan | Campus at Alte Akademie 8; can read visually as science / gardens / hill campus. | [TUM campus contact](https://www.tum.de/ueber-die-tum/kontakt-und-anfahrt) |
| Weihenstephan Brewery | Historic brewery on Weihenstephan hill, embedded among the TUM science campus. | [Weihenstephan Brewery](https://www.weihenstephaner.de/en/our-brewery) |
| Füssen / Neuschwanstein | Alpine foothill and castle day-trip pair; treat them as a distant vignette, not Munich city-centre stickers. | [Neuschwanstein official site](https://www.schloss-neuschwanstein.de/englisch/tourist/index.htm) |
| Reutte | Austrian alpine / river / mountain context; must remain visually beyond the Füssen cluster. | [Naturparkregion Reutte](https://www.reutte.com/de) |
